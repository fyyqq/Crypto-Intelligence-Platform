# Tools

**Routes:** `/tools` (landing) · `/tools/market-indicators` · `/tools/market-charts` · `/tools/crypto-calendar` · `/tools/cpi-fomc` · **Status:** Live (2026-10-05); split into sub-pages 2026-10-14 (**local only, not deployed**).
**Source:** `app/services/market_tools_service.py`, `frontend/frontend/state/tools_state.py`, `frontend/frontend/components/tools_page.py` (`TOOL_PAGES`, `tools_home_content`, `market_indicators_content`, `market_charts_content`), `frontend/frontend/frontend.py` (`tools_page`, `market_indicators_page`, `market_charts_page`, `_dropdown_nav_item`), card styles `.tool-card` in `assets/styles.css`.

**Structure (2026-10-14).** `/tools` used to hold everything on one long page; it is now a clean landing page: the title "Market Tools" and a two-column grid (stacked on phones) with one large link card per tool: Market Indicators, Market Charts and **Gainers & Losers** (3rd card, links to `/gainers-losers`, which has no navbar link of its own since 2026-10-14) and **Crypto Calendar** (4th card, `/tools/crypto-calendar`). Each card (a real `<a>`) has an accent-tinted icon tile, the title, a one-sentence description, chips naming what is inside, and an "Open ->" footer; on hover it lifts, gets an accent border and the arrows nudge. The list is the single `TOOL_PAGES` list, which also feeds the header's **Tools dropdown** (hover, like News): Market Indicators, Market Charts, Gainers & Losers, Crypto Calendar, CPI & FOMC. "Tools" stays highlighted on every sub-page, and each sub-page has a "<- Market Tools" back link above its title.
- **`/tools/market-indicators`** (Market Indicators): Fear & Greed and Altcoin / Bitcoin Season side by side (one column below 1280px), then the full-width BTC / ETH Rainbow Chart, with the data-source popup. Loads `ToolsState.refresh_fng`, `refresh_season`, `refresh_rainbow` (which starts the rainbow live loop) and `rainbow_hover.js`; the landing page and the charts page load none of that.
- **`/tools/market-charts`** (Market Charts): the five TradingView embeds in a 2-column grid (DXY, total market cap, BTC dominance, ETH/BTC, TOTAL2), each with its own 1m-W timeframe buttons (`tradingview_timeframes.js` is global). No ToolsState events.
- **`/tools/crypto-calendar`** (Crypto Calendar; built 2026-10-14): the monthly "Crypto + Market Calendar" posts of the Telegram group **@layergg** as a full-width month calendar. Source/code: `app/services/crypto_calendar_service.py` (parser + HTML), `state/calendar_state.py`, `tools_page.py::crypto_calendar_content`, styles `.cal-*` in `assets/styles.css`, tests `tests/test_crypto_calendar.py`, one-off `scripts/backfill_layergg_calendar.py`. **Data:** no new table: the posts are `news_articles` rows (source_type `telegram`, source_name `Layergg...`, title with "Calendar"/"캘린더", not "Weekly") stored by the Telegram news pipeline; the page parses their *text* on load (cached 5 min), so a new month's post appears by itself once the listener stores it. The image attached to each post holds more events than the text (about 80 vs 16-24); only the text is used. **Parser:** one "noteworthy events" line per event in three formats seen so far (`10-03 - $HYPE - text`, `Sep 1: US - text`, Korean `8월 1일: $CRCL(@circle) - text`), then an undated TBD/TBA list; intro bullets, other-month dates and everything after the footer ("See detail", disclaimer, hashtags) are ignored; 🔥 marks important events. Each event gets a colour like the source image: **crypto** green, **macro** orange (country/central-bank/agency names, CPI/FOMC/GDP/bond words), **stocks & earnings** blue (earnings words and listed companies such as NVDA/HOOD). **UI:** month title with `‹ ›` arrows, month chips from January 2026 to the current month (dimmed when that month has no post; it opens on the latest month that has events), colour legend, then a Monday-Friday week grid across the full width (empty cells for days outside the month, today outlined; event cards with a CoinMarketCap logo on crypto tickers, bold ticker, text), a "Weekend events" list (cards with their date), a "Date to be announced" list and a link to that month's post on t.me. Below 768px the grid becomes a day-by-day list (empty days hidden). Event text is HTML-escaped. **Which posts count:** every monthly post starts with the 🗓 emoji, is titled "... Calendar" (or Korean "캘린더"), ends with `#Calendar #Schedule` and "See detail @Layergg" and carries an image; the title wording varies ("2026 Crypto January Calendar", "2026 Market May Calendar", "2026 June Crypto + Market Calendar"), so nothing requires the words "Crypto" or "Market", and the service SQL is just `title ~ calendar|캘린더` minus "weekly" (the 🤡 "Weekly Crypto Calendar Highlight" posts are not parsed yet). A month's post is often published the day before (the January 2026 one on 31 Dec 2025), so the month and year come from the title, not the post date. **Year:** the page shows the latest year that has a parsed post (not a fixed 2026): 2026 stays until the first 2027 post exists (even published on 31 Dec), then only 2027 is shown, from January (`view_year`, `month_keys`); chips run from January to the current month, or to a later month whose post is already out. **Live refresh:** while the tab is open `CalendarState.watch` re-checks every 60 s (server cache 45 s): a new or edited post updates the chips, and if the visitor is on the newest month with events ("following") the new month becomes the active tab and is shown by itself; a visitor reading an older month stays on it and only gets the new chip. Verified by inserting temporary November and December posts into the local database with the page open (no reload, about 50 s each), then deleting them. **Coverage (local and live):** Jan-Oct 2026, all ten months (Jan 13 events, Feb 24, Mar 17, Apr 25, May 15, Jun 20, Jul 16, Aug 15 + 3 TBA, Sep 14 + 2, Oct 16 + 8; a 2025 December post is stored but not shown). Everything before August was older than the news pipeline's 30-day window, so it was fetched once with `python -m scripts.backfill_layergg_calendar [YYYY-MM-DD]` (default 2025-12-01; read-only, through the pipeline's own `store_message`; stop the Telegram listener first, restart it after) and copied to the live database. The first version of that script required "Crypto" in the title and started on 1 Jan, which is why January and May were missed. The card on `/tools` and the dropdown entry link here.
- **`/tools/cpi-fomc`** (shown as **Forex Factory** in the Tools dropdown, the `/tools` card and the page heading "Forex Factory Calendar" since 2026-10-15; the URL kept its old slug; built 2026-10-15): this week's US inflation (CPI), Federal Reserve (FOMC) and **Non-Farm Employment Change (NFP, added 2026-10-15)** events with exact date and time, impact, actual / forecast / previous, updating live. Code: `app/services/macro_calendar_service.py`, `state/macro_calendar_state.py`, `tools_page.py::cpi_fomc_content`, styles `.mc-*`, tests `tests/test_macro_calendar.py`. **Sources (all free, no trial):** (1) *schedule, forecast, previous*: the public Forex Factory calendar feed `https://nfs.faireconomy.media/ff_calendar_thisweek.json` (no key; fields title, currency, ISO date with the New York offset, impact, forecast, previous; **no actual or revised values**; this week only, the next-week file does not exist). Forex Factory itself has no API and blocks scripted page requests, so its pages are never read. The feed is **rate limited** (HTTP 429 "Rate Limited", `Retry-After` about 5 minutes after a handful of requests: it hit the dev machine while testing), so it is fetched at most once every 3 minutes for all viewers, a `Retry-After` is obeyed, and the last good copy is kept in `logs/macro_feed.json` (survives restarts and blocks; shown as "last copy" for up to 6 h). (2) *actual*: the official US sources, looked up on a background thread (never blocking the page) only after a release and only for events still empty, at most every 2 minutes and 20 times a day: **CPI from the BLS public API** (v1, no key; one POST for the four CPI series; `CPI m/m` and `Core CPI m/m` from the seasonally adjusted index, `y/y` from the not adjusted one, one decimal, for the month before the release; months BLS lists as "-" are skipped) and the **Federal Funds Rate** decision from FRED's public CSV of the target's upper bound (FRED can lag a day; it stalls browser-style User-Agents, so that request sends the HTTP library's default). Found actuals are kept in `logs/macro_actuals.json`. September 2026 CPI (released 14 Oct) was filled in 25 s after the feed came back: m/m 0.6%, y/y 3.7%, core m/m 0.3%, core y/y 2.6%. (3) *optional extra*: the Apify actor `scrapemint/forexfactory-economic-calendar` (pay per event, $0.01 per row, the first 25 rows of every run free) when `APIFY_TOKEN` is set in `.env`: a run asks for USD, high and medium impact, this week (under 25 rows, so normally free), and only for released events the free sources could not fill, at most once per 3 minutes and `MACRO_ACTUALS_MAX_RUNS_PER_DAY` (40) a day. **Not verified against the real actor** (no token was available): the row fields are read tolerantly (title/event, currency, actual, revised), and any failure is logged and ignored. **What is shown:** only events whose title has `CPI`, `FOMC`, `Federal Funds Rate`, `Fed Chair` or `Non-Farm Employment Change` (so the FOMC members' speeches, the rate decision, the statement/minutes/press conference, the Fed Chair, and the monthly jobs report; green NFP chip). The NFP actual ("29K") is the month's change in BLS total nonfarm payrolls (series CES0000000001, thousands, this month's level minus the revised previous month's), filled in like CPI after the release. **Optional BLS key:** `BLS_API_KEY` in `.env` (free registration at https://data.bls.gov/registrationEngine/, a key emailed and activated by link) switches the BLS request from v1 (25 requests a day) to v2 (500 a day, `registrationkey` in the POST); without it everything still works. "USD only" (default) or "All currencies" (e.g. China, eurozone CPI). Times are Kuala Lumpur with the New York time under each, grouped by KL day, past rows dimmed, the next 30 minutes highlighted, a "NEXT EVENT" card with a countdown (the next high-impact event, else the next one), animated dots while an actual is expected, a neutral ▲/▼ when the actual is above/below the forecast, and a clear message (not "no events") while the feed is blocked and no copy exists. `MacroCalendarState.watch` re-reads every 30 s while the tab is open (verified: it recovered from a rate-limit block by itself, without a reload). Phones get a labelled card per event.
The homepage Market Status card's title now links to `/tools/market-indicators`. Verified in light and dark at 1440 / 1024 / 834 / 375: no overflow, no horizontal scroll, zero console errors; the old content is gone from `/tools` (no iframes, gauge or rainbow there).

## Features

<details><summary>Fear & Greed Index</summary>

A semicircle gauge (server-built SVG, 5 colour zones), the value and label in the zone's colour, and Yesterday / Last week / Last month values. **Sources** (chosen in the "Data source" popup): Alternative.me (free, no key, default) or CoinMarketCap (`COINMARKETCAP_API_KEY`, 1-2 credits per refresh). Cached 15 minutes in-process.
</details>

<details><summary>Altcoin / Bitcoin Season</summary>

Index 0-100 = share of the top 50 coins (stablecoins, wrapped/staked/bridged tokens excluded) that beat Bitcoin over the window; 75+ = Altcoin Season, 25 or less = Bitcoin Season, otherwise Neutral. Shows a marker bar, "N of 50 beat Bitcoin", and the 5 best performers. **Sources**: CoinMarketCap 90 days (standard method, needs `COINMARKETCAP_API_KEY`, 1 credit per refresh) or CoinGecko 30 days (free, no key; CoinGecko's free tier has no 90-day figure, so it reacts faster). Cached 30 minutes. blockchaincenter.net was not used: it offers no public chart widget or data API (only a share-card image of the gauge), and embedding their whole page was tried and removed (their header/ads came with it; cropping it to just the chart would be fragile). CoinMarketCap, Coinglass and Alternative.me block iframes.
</details>

<details><summary>BTC / ETH Rainbow Chart</summary>

Drawn server-side as SVG from daily USD prices: **CoinMetrics' free community API** (BTC from 18 Jul 2010, ETH from Aug 2015; no key), with Yahoo Finance (BTC from 2014) as fallback; cached 6 hours. **Bitcoin** uses the widely used logarithmic-growth curve as the centre, `log10(price) = 2.66167 * ln(days since 2009-01-09) - 17.9183`, with nine bands spanning +/-0.6 decades around it (this is what Blockchaincenter/Coinglass-style charts use; an earlier version fitted its own regression on 2014+ data with wider bands and so looked different, with BTC reading "Still cheap" instead of "Fire sale"). **Ethereum** has no standard curve, so its centre is a least-squares fit of its own history and its bands span the historical envelope around that fit. The bands are extended about 1.5 years ahead; the header shows the current price and band; BTC/ETH tabs. Labels and colours follow the common rainbow charts (Fire sale ... Maximum bubble territory).
</details>

<details><summary>Rainbow chart: live price and hover tooltip (2026-10-13)</summary>

**Live price.** The history (CoinMetrics daily closes) only goes up to yesterday's close, so the headline price used to be that close (cached 6h). Now `market_tools_service._live_spot` reads the spot USD price from free public exchange tickers, no key: Coinbase, then Kraken, then Binance (USDT); cached 10s. `ToolsState.rainbow_live_loop` re-reads it every 15s while the page is open and moves the price (through `_fmt_usd`), the band badge, a "Live · <time> UTC · <source>" line with a pulsing dot, and the chart's last segment + live dot. The chart itself (`rainbow_svg`, cached 6h) is not re-sent: the live part is a tiny overlay SVG with the same viewBox stacked on top (`rainbow_live()` -> `rainbow_overlay`, `.rainbow-overlay`). If every ticker fails, it falls back to the last daily close ("as of <date> (daily close)").

**Hover / tap tooltip** (free, our own code, no library or service): `assets/rainbow_hover.js` reads the chart model sent once per asset in `data-rainbow` on `.rainbow-chart` (geometry, band model `slope`/`icpt`/`lo`/`step`, and every daily close). On hover (or tap on touch screens) it draws a vertical guide, a dot on the price line, and a card with the date, that day's price, and all nine bands' price levels at that date with the price's band highlighted. Future dates show "Projected bands". Prices in the card mirror `_fmt_usd`; band levels are compact ($46.1K) like the axis. Touch: the card stays until a tap outside the chart; vertical scrolling still works (`touch-action: pan-y`). Theme tokens only (`.rainbow-tip` in styles.css).
</details>

<details><summary>CoinMarketCap fallback for Fear & Greed and Season (2026-10-13)</summary>

When the chosen source is CoinMarketCap and it fails (rate limit / monthly credits used up / key rejected / unreachable), the card shows the free source's result instead (Alternative.me for Fear & Greed, CoinGecko 30 days for Season) with an amber note "CoinMarketCap unavailable (<reason>), showing <source> instead." A failed CMC source is not retried for 10 minutes (`_with_cmc_fallback`, `_CMC_FAIL_TTL`).
</details>

<details><summary>TradingView embeds: DXY, total market cap, BTC dominance, ETH/BTC, TOTAL2</summary>

Free `widgetembed` iframes (same mechanism as the coin page chart), daily candles by default, KL timezone, light/dark via `rx.color_mode_cond`. Each chart has independent 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, D, and W controls. Symbols: `CAPITALCOM:DXY`, `CRYPTOCAP:TOTAL`, `CRYPTOCAP:BTC.D`, `BINANCE:ETHBTC`, `CRYPTOCAP:TOTAL2`. **`TVC:DXY` (the usual DXY symbol) is blocked in embeds** ("only available on TradingView"), so DXY uses Capital.com's index, which tracks the same value. No key needed.
</details>

<details><summary>Data source popup and API keys</summary>

Fear & Greed and Season each have a "Data source" button that opens a popup listing the sources with a badge: "No key needed", "Key found in .env", or "Key missing". A source with a key shows the exact line to add (`NAME=your_key` in the project-root `.env`). **"Re-check .env"** re-reads the file, so a newly added key works without restarting the app. The popup opens by itself, and the widget shows an "API key needed" card, when the viewer's chosen source has no key. The choice is saved per browser (localStorage `repace_tools_fng_source` / `repace_tools_season_source`); with no choice the first usable source is used. `.env` is trusted over the process environment when the file exists (the app loads it at startup, so the environment would keep reporting a removed key).
</details>

## Verified live

All eight sections render with live data (F&G 65 Greed, Season 64 Neutral with 32/50, BTC $85.3K "Still cheap", ETH tab works, five charts load, DXY 101.56); switching F&G to CoinMarketCap changes the source line; with the CMC key removed from `.env` the popup opened by itself with "Key missing", and after restoring it "Re-check" cleared it with no restart; zero console errors.

## Known gaps

Rainbow-chart bands are our own fit. The Season index is not blockchaincenter's exact figure. The key popup only explains where to put the key; keys are never typed into the page or written by the app.

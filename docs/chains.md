# Chains

**Route:** `/chains` · **Status:** Index of every chain (2026-10-15)
**Source:** `frontend/frontend/frontend.py::chains_page`, `components/index_pages.py`

Shows a "Chains" heading with a count and **every chain as a pill, all on one page (no pagination)** — the same list the homepage "More Chain" pages through (`CoinState.chain_names`). Clicking a pill selects that chain in the homepage filter and goes to `/` (`CoinState.open_chain`).

**Table (2026-10-15, replaces the pill list):** columns `# | Chain | Top coins | Biggest gainer | Biggest loser`, **10 rows a page** (`CoinState.narrative_board`/`chain_board`: the full table stays on the server, the browser gets one page; page 1 on every visit). Top coins = its 10 largest coins by market cap as logo + ticker pills (link to the coin page); biggest gainer / loser = its 3 coins with the largest 24h rise / fall among ALL its coins with a 24h change (only coins that actually rose / fell, with their %), "–" when none or while the 24h window is still collecting. Code: `components/index_pages.py`, `sector_performance_service.sector_table`. Ranking: rows are sorted by each chain's 24h score, shown at the right of its name (green up / red down): the plain average 24h change of its **top 20 coins by market cap** that have a 24h change (at least 3, otherwise no number and listed after the scored ones by size). Changes come from our own hourly `price_snapshot` rows (top 500 coins, under $50K volume left out; same data as Gainers & Losers), so it costs **no API call**; the result is cached for every session and recomputed at most **hourly** (the snapshots only change hourly; 10 min while the 24h window is still collecting). Code: `app/services/sector_performance_service.py` (tests `tests/test_sector_performance.py`), `coin_state.sector_boards` / `CoinState.load_sector_boards`, `components/index_pages.py`.

**Sorting, search and stablecoins (2026-10-15):** the *Biggest gainer* and *Biggest loser* headers have a sort arrow (click: biggest first (down arrow) -> smallest first (up arrow) -> back to the 24h ranking; a loser counts by the size of its fall; rows without a gainer/loser go last); a search box on the right of the explanatory line (that line is a full-width row, text left, box right, stacked on phones) filters by chain name, keeps each row's number and shows "No chain matches". No chain is removed. Sort and search reset on every visit (`CoinState.cycle_chain_sort`, `set_chain_search`, `coin_state.sector_view`).

**Not to be confused with:** the [Home](./home.md) page's own chain pill filter — that's a Home feature. An early planning note in this project's Notion workspace called this idea the "Blockchain Page" — scope not yet decided.
